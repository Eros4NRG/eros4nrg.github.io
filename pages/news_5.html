Eros4NRG launches Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for Next-Gen Energy analytics

Martel Innovate is proud to announce the successful completion of the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for Eros4NRG, a cutting-edge platform designed to revolutionize energy data monitoring and analytics. This milestone marks a significant step forward in addressing key challenges in energy management, including unreliable IoT data, disorganized energy datasets, and the need for actionable insights for stakeholders.


Key Features of the Eros4NRG MVP


The MVP comprises four core modules, each playing a critical role in transforming raw energy data into actionable intelligence:

  • Data Gathering Module

    Continuously collects real-time data from electric vehicles (EVs), EV charging stations, photovoltaic (PV) plants, and smart headquarters via APIs. Stores raw data in a scalable MinIO data lake, ensuring efficient processing and retrieval.

  • Data Processing Module

    Transforms raw data into structured formats for analysis. Utilizes PostgreSQL for reliable storage, enabling advanced querying and AI/ML model training.

  • Data Visualization Module

    Provides an intuitive dashboard for monitoring energy metrics such as battery status, charging sessions, and consumption trends. Detects anomalies (e.g., sensor errors) and highlights usage patterns to support decision-making.

  • Data Catalogue Module

    Offers a FastAPI-based interface for querying cleaned data, making it accessible for stakeholders

Real-World Impact

The MVP has already demonstrated its value by uncovering data inconsistencies, such as irregular battery percentages in EV fleets, enabling proactive maintenance. The platform’s modular architecture ensures scalability, paving the way for future enhancements like AI-driven predictive analytics and zero-trust security.
