Eros4NRG: A One-Stop Shop for Energy Data Analytics

The complexity of energy data is increasing, and stakeholders require smarter tools to manage, analyze, and act on this information efficiently. Eros4NRG is positioned as a One-Stop Shop (OSS) for energy data analytics, simplifying the way distribution system operators (DSOs), EV fleet managers, and other energy stakeholders access and leverage data.


By combining Zero Trust Architectures (ZTA), Explainable AI (xAI), and predictive analytics, Eros4NRG enables:

  • Secure and Reliable Energy Data Management: Ensuring the integrity and security of IoT and edge device data.
  • Enhanced Predictive Analysis: Helping stakeholders anticipate energy demand, optimize fleet usage, and improve grid management.
  • Improved Decision-Making: Providing transparent and actionable insights tailored to industry needs.

For example, ASM Terni, a key partner in the project, is using Eros4NRG to enhance grid management through better forecasting and data-driven decision-making. Similarly, EMOTION, representing EV fleets, benefits from improved vehicle data analysis to optimize fleet operations and charging strategies.
