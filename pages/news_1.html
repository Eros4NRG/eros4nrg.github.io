Eros4NRG receives funding to enhance Energy Data Security

We are thrilled to announce that Eros4NRG has been selected as a winner in Open Call #1 of the Horizon Europe NEMO project! This funding marks the official launch of Eros4NRG, an initiative designed to enhance trust and transparency in energy data ecosystems.
As part of the NEMO Smart Energy Trial (Smart Energy and Mobility Living Lab), Eros4NRG integrates Zero Trust Architectures (ZTA) and Explainable AI (xAI) to address key challenges in IoT/Edge data reliability for smart grids. Our work will directly support stakeholders such as ASM Terni (DSO) and EMOTION (EV fleets), empowering them with secure, data-driven decision-making tools.
With the project officially launched, we look forward to collaborating with partners, refining our technology, and delivering innovative solutions to the European energy sector. Stay tuned for further updates on our progress!
