Behind the scenes: Building the Eros4NRG plugin for NEMO

The Eros4NRG plugin is a key component in integrating the project with the NEMO Meta-Operating System, ensuring seamless data sharing, security and analysis within smart energy ecosystems


Development Highlights:


  • Predictive Analytics Engine: Designed to process IoT and edge data, enabling smarter forecasting for energy and mobility applications.
  • Zero Trust Integration: Implementing continuous verification mechanisms to enhance cybersecurity across the energy data chain.
  • Interoperability with NEMO: Ensuring that Eros4NRG functions smoothly as part of the Smart Energy and Mobility Living Lab.

By refining data models and continuously integrating stakeholder feedback, Eros4NRG is ensuring its plugin is both practical and impactful in real-world applications. Stay tuned for updates on our development journey!
