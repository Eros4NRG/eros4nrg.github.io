Real-world impact: How Eros4NRG enhances Smart Energy and EV Fleet management

Eros4NRG is already making strides in improving energy management and EV fleet optimization. With stakeholders such as ASM Terni and EMOTION, the project is shaping data-driven strategies that enhance decision-making and operational efficiency.


  • ASM Terni (DSO*): By leveraging Eros4NRG’s predictive analytics, ASM Terni can better forecast energy consumption and distribution needs, ensuring a more reliable and optimized grid.
  • EMOTION (EV Fleets): Fleet managers benefit from real-time insights into vehicle data, optimizing charging schedules and energy consumption to improve overall fleet performance.

Through secure, transparent and explainable AI, Eros4NRG ensures stakeholders can trust the data they use to make critical energy decisions. As the project progresses, continuous refinements will further enhance its ability to predict, analyze and optimize energy usage in smart grids and mobility systems.



*DSO=Distributed System Operator
