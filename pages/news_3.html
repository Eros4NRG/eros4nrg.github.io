Eros4NRG Unveils Detailed Design for Next-Gen Energy Analytics Platform

Martel Innovate is excited to announce the publications of D1 – "Eros4NRG Detailed Design", a foundational deliverable that lays the architectural blueprint for the Eros4NRG platform. This milestone marks a critical step toward realizing a One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for energy stakeholders, enabling data-driven decision-making through predictive analytics and secure, scalable infrastructure.


Key Highlights of the Detailed Design


The D1 deliverable outlines Eros4NRG’s vision to address pressing challenges in energy data management, including unreliable IoT sources, disorganized datasets, and the need for AI/ML transparency. Key components of the design include:


Zero Trust Data Services

  • Secure data capture from IoT/Edge devices (e.g. EV fleets, PV plants).
  • Anomaly detection and workflow orchestration to ensure data quality.
  • Integration with cloud-native tools like Kubernetes and Docker.

Collaborative Data Enclaves

  • A multi-layered storage system featuring a Data Lake, Graph Enclave (for metadata), and Dynamic Enclave (for time-series data).
  • Semantic links between static and dynamic data to enhance query efficiency.

One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for Energy

  • User-friendly dashboards for real-time visualizations and AI/ML-driven forecasts.
  • Role-based access control via NEMO Unified Access Control to secure sensitive data.
  • Predictive models for PV energy production, building consumption, and EV battery health.

Pilot Applications & Impact


Eros4NRG’s design caters to two key stakeholders:

  • ASM Terni: Optimizing demand-response strategies with 3-hour-ahead energy forecasts.
  • EMOTION: Monitoring EV charging patterns and predicting battery state of charge (1-hour-ahead).

The platform’s economic potential spans energy operators, IT SMEs, researchers and policymakers, with projected benefits including:

  • 5–10% energy savings through AI-driven insights
  • 50% reduction in data maintenance efforts via automated anomaly detection.
  • Compliance with EU data spaces (e.g., GAIA-X) for secure cross-sector collaboration.

With the design phase finalized, the Eros4NRG team will proceed to WP2 – "DEVELOP", where the core modules will be built and tested. The upcoming D2 deliverable will showcase the Minimum Viable Product (MVP), featuring early integrations and pilot validations.
